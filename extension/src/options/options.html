Deals Analyzer

Rates deals from strong no to strong yes by having Claude search current prices for the same and similar products. It runs by itself on eDealinfo.com; on any store's product page, click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+D.

The extension doesn't have access to websites and api.anthropic.com yet. It needs them to show the Deal check tab on product pages and to reach Claude.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. It's stored only in this browser and only sent to api.anthropic.com. Testing it is free.

Each search costs $0.01. With Haiku, a typical analysis costs about $0.02–$0.07 in total.

Claude writes its verdicts and answers in this language. The panel's labels and this page are translated once when you save (a fraction of a cent), then kept. Prices are still compared at US stores, in dollars.

Simple has Claude write a few dozen words instead of a few hundred, so the rating comes sooner and costs a little less. Follow-up questions work in both.

On by default. A separate search that runs alongside the analysis, so the rating isn't slowed. About $0.02–$0.03 the first time for a store and brand, then free for 24 hours on any product from them. Discounts come from the store's or brand's own pages and verification services (ID.me, SheerID, UNiDAYS); dead pages are dropped, but codes and who qualifies aren't tested, so check at checkout.

Puts a badge under each product on pages that list many (search results, categories, deal lists) with its rating and how its price compares with the same item and an equivalent product elsewhere. Products are rated as you scroll to them, 3 at a time and at most 40 per page, with the simple verdict and up to 3 searches each: about $0.03 per product, so a page of 20 costs about $0.60. Products rated in the last 24 hours are free. Click a badge for the full panel.

Each new product costs about $0.05, so browsing many adds up; revisiting one within 24 hours is free, and tabs opened in the background wait until you view them. Turn this off to analyze only when you click.

On by default. Compared prices are read from each store's page. Saved results

Verdicts are kept for 24 hours so revisiting a deal is free. Use "Re-analyze" in the panel to refresh one.

When you analyze a deal, its details (title, price, store, model number) are sent to Anthropic's API, which runs the web searches. On a store page that includes up to 2,500 characters of the product section's text, with your name and delivery location removed. With automatic analysis on, this happens for every product page you view; turn it off and it happens only when you click. Compared stores' pages are read by your browser, and only the prices go to Claude. The discount search sends the product's name, brand, store and price; rating a list page sends each product's name, price, link and a short excerpt of its tile. Translating sends only the extension's own words. Nothing else from your browsing is sent.
