Deals Analyzer

Rates deals from strong no to strong yes by having Claude search current prices for the same and similar products. It runs by itself on eDealinfo.com; on any store's product page, click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+D.

The extension doesn't have access to websites and api.anthropic.com yet. It needs them to show the Deal check tab on product pages and to reach Claude.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. It's stored only in this browser and only sent to api.anthropic.com. Testing it is free.

Each search costs $0.01. With Haiku, a typical analysis costs about $0.02–$0.07 in total.

On by default: every product page you look at, on any store or deal site, is checked with no click needed. Each new product costs about $0.05, so browsing many adds up; revisiting one within 24 hours is free, and tabs opened in the background wait until you view them. Turn this off to analyze only when you click.

On by default. Off keeps the extension off store pages, automatic checks included, except eDealinfo. The toolbar button and Alt+Shift+D work either way.

On by default. Compared prices are read from each store's page. Most stores allow that in the background; a few (eBay, for example) only load as a real page, so a muted tab opens behind the current one for a few seconds and closes itself. It's a normal visit, so it may appear in that site's recently viewed items. Off, those prices are marked "not confirmed".

Saved results

Verdicts are kept for 24 hours so revisiting a deal is free. Use "Re-analyze" in the panel to refresh one.

When you analyze a deal, its details (title, price, store, model number) are sent to Anthropic's API, which runs the web searches. On a store page that includes up to 2,500 characters of the product section's text, with your name and delivery location removed. With automatic analysis on, this happens for every product page you view; turn it off and it happens only when you click. Compared stores' pages are read by your browser, and only the prices go to Claude. Nothing else from your browsing is sent.
