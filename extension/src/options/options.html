Deals Analyzer

Rates deals on eDealinfo.com from strong no to strong yes by having Claude search current prices for the same and similar products.

The extension doesn't have access to eDealinfo.com and api.anthropic.com yet.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. It's stored only in this browser and only sent to api.anthropic.com. Testing it is free.

Each search costs $0.01. With Haiku, a typical analysis costs about $0.02–$0.07 in total.

Saved results

Verdicts are kept for 24 hours so revisiting a deal is free. Use "Re-analyze" in the panel to refresh one.

When you analyze a deal, its details (title, price, store, model number) are sent to Anthropic's API, which runs the web searches. Nothing else from your browsing is sent.
